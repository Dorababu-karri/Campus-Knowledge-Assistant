# University Academic Integrity Policy

## Section 1: Introduction
This document outlines the standard academic integrity policies for the university. All students are expected to uphold the highest standards of honesty.

## Section 2: Plagiarism
Plagiarism is defined as presenting someone else's work, words, or ideas as your own without proper attribution.

## Section 3: Penalties for Plagiarism
If a student is caught plagiarizing, they will immediately receive a failing grade for the assignment. A second offense will result in academic suspension for one semester. A third offense results in permanent expulsion.

## Section 4: AI Assistance
The use of artificial intelligence tools to generate essays or assignments without explicit permission from the instructor is strictly prohibited and falls under the category of academic dishonesty.
